## Making shapes - squares and triangles

### Square
def draw_square(size):
    for i in range(size):
         print("🧱" * size)
    
draw_square(4)

### Triangle

def draw_triangle(rows):
     for i in range(rows):
        print("🧱" * (i + 1))

draw_triangle(4)

### Right aligned triangle

def draw_triangle(size):
    for i in range(size):
        print(" " * (size - i) + "#" * (i+1))

draw_triangle(5)
