## My Project Proposal

**What I'm building:** 

I'm building a song lyrics analyzer. Using this text analysis tool, I want to plug in lyrics from artists I listen to and identify patterns in their vocabulary, themes, and writing style.

**Why I chose this:** 

I chose this idea because I have a strong interest in music, both listening and creating. I am intersted in the product side, but I also want to explore the lyrical side more (for personal use and to work with others). I think it would be interesting to analyze how different artists use language in their songs and whether certain words or patterns can be assocated with specific genres. 

**Core features:** 

- Load lyrics from a text file (multiple songs, multiple artists)
- Clean and process the text (like lowercase, remove punctuation, filter common words)
- Count word frequencies using dictionaries
- Compare vocabulary across songs or artists (like most used words, unique word count)
- Display the results of top words, total words, and vocabulary richness

**What I don't know yet:** 

- What all I will have to do to clear and filter text (especially removing stop words properly)
- How to structure comparisons across multiple files or artists
- How I will define and measure "vocabulary richness"
- Maybe if there is a way to make a visualization?
