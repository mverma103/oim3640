I also made 2 iterations of the game: casino.py and casino_final.py. casino.py was a test module where I first started and just went 1 game at a time with what made sense in my mind, just simple if/else statements to complete the whole game. the second iteration (casino_final.py) restructured the entire code to have functions and a much more simple way of coding/understanding the game. I was able to include more features (like a betting history, for loops for terminal effects, etc.) and most importantly I got to improve on everything I wanted to in python.
